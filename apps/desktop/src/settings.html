Controls

Hotkey
The key you use to dictate, or press Record shortcut for a combination such as Ctrl+Alt+D. A laptop Copilot key works too: tap it to start and tap again to stop.
Command Mode shortcut
Select text in any app, hold this shortcut and say what to do, such as “make this shorter”, “translate to English” or “fix the grammar”. The selection is replaced.
How it listens
Hold to talk records while the key is down. A quick tap keeps recording until you tap again. The other mode always starts on one press and stops on the next.
Microphone
If the chosen one is unplugged, Flow uses the system default.
Floating icon
Drag it anywhere. Click it to open these settings.
Sound cues
A short chime when recording starts (you can speak) and when it stops.
Mute sound while dictating
Silences music and videos while you speak, then restores the volume. Windows only.
Start with Windows
Launch Flow quietly in the tray when you sign in.

Tone by app

      Snippets

      Say the trigger and Flow types the expansion exactly as written. “New line”, “new paragraph”, “delete that”, “select all”, “undo”, “press enter”, “press tab” and “press escape” already work. Say “press enter” after your text to send it.

        Writing style

        A short description of how you write. Flow follows it when it tidies what you said.

        Auto cleanup

        Choose how much Flow edits after transcription. Applies to every app.

        Light removes fillers and fixes grammar with minimal rewriting.

        Hindi

        Choose how spoken Hindi is written. English stays English, and other languages are written as spoken.
